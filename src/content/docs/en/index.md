TalePort is where you write a branching story, get it reviewed and sell it to readers. These pages are the manual, and each part of the app has its own page.

<div class="home-actions">

[Start with the video lessons](/en/getting-started/video-lessons/)
[Read the Content Rules](/en/publishing/content-rules/)

</div>

<div class="home-route">

1. **[Write](/en/story-editor/story-graph/)** Build your story from nodes joined by choices.
2. **[Review](/en/publishing/review-process/)** A reviewer reads your chapter and pins notes to your nodes.
3. **[Publish](/en/getting-started/publishing/)** Once approved, the chapter goes live. Early-access readers get it first, and everyone else gets it seven days later.
4. **[Earn](/en/monetization/requirements/)** A paid chapter costs 9 to 299 EPS. Royalties are paid out monthly.

</div>
