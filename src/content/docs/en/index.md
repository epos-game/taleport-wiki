TalePort is where you write a branching story, put it through review and sell it to readers. These pages are the manual for all of it: one page per thing you touch in the app, with the rule as the app actually enforces it. So when a chapter refuses to go to review because its only ending closes the story flow instead of being marked **End of chapter**, the answer is here.

<div class="home-actions">

[Start with the video lessons](/en/getting-started/video-lessons/)
[Read the Content Rules](/en/publishing/content-rules/)

</div>

<div class="home-route">

1. **[Write](/en/story-editor/story-graph/)** Nodes on a graph, joined by choices.
2. **[Review](/en/publishing/review-process/)** A reviewer reads the chapter and pins notes to your nodes.
3. **[Publish](/en/getting-started/publishing/)** Approval makes it live: early access first, everyone else seven days later.
4. **[Earn](/en/monetization/requirements/)** A paid chapter costs between 9 and 299 EPS. Royalties are paid out monthly.

</div>
