Your story has a **story link** that opens it in the EPOS app. Find it on the story's detail page under **Link to Epos app**, with a QR code and a copy button.

The link appears once one chapter is in testing, in review or published. A story with only drafts has no link.

![the story link panel on a story detail page, showing the link, its copy action and the QR code](/screens/en/getting-started/story-link-panel.png)

## Stability of the link

The address never changes. You can rename the story, add chapters or move it to another author profile, and the link still works.

An **Active** badge sits next to the address. **Copy QR** copies the code to your clipboard as an image.

## Link uses

- Launch posts and video descriptions. The address keeps working, so you can paste it anywhere.
- Finding testers. The link alone isn't enough. You also need to add the testers as contributors, otherwise they can't see a chapter that is only in testing.

## Access by role

What the link opens depends on who clicks it.

- **Readers** see the chapters released to them. Early access decides when. If you approved a chapter without a scheduled date, early access starts at approval, and general availability follows 7 days later. Backers at the Visionary tier and above get early access. Founder-tier backers and everyone else wait those 7 days.
- **You and your contributors** get every chapter that isn't a draft. The chapters are free for you, with no age gate, and the early access date is ignored. This is how a test build reaches your testers.
- **Nobody** gets a draft. To share unfinished work, put the chapter into testing and add the person as a contributor. See [Testing and contributors](/en/getting-started/testing-and-contributors/).
