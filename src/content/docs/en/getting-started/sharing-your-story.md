Every story that has left the drafting stage gets a **story link**: one URL on its detail page in TalePort that opens that story inside the EPOS app, with a QR code and a copy button beside it. It gives you a single thing to paste under a trailer, print on a flyer or read out on a stream. Put the QR code on the last slide of a reading of *The Salt Road* and the room has the story open on their phones before anyone leaves.

The link appears as soon as one chapter is in testing, in review or published. A story where everything is still a draft has no link, because nothing has been built yet for it to open.

![the story link panel on a story detail page, showing the link, its copy action and the QR code](/screens/en/getting-started/story-link-panel.png)

## It does not change

The URL is built from the story's id and nothing else. In production it reads `https://epos.games/app/story/<story id>`, and it stays that way for the story's whole life. Retitling the story, adding chapters, moving it to another author profile: none of it rewrites the link. You can hand it to a festival programme months before the last chapter is written and it will still be the right link afterwards.

Next to the address the panel shows an **Active** badge. *Copy QR* puts the code on your clipboard as an image, ready for a slide, a poster or a stream overlay.

## What it is good for

- Launch posts and video descriptions, where you want one address you will never have to correct.
- Recruiting testers. Send the link, but add the people as contributors too, or a chapter that is only in testing will not be there for them.

## What people find at the other end

That depends on who clicks.

Readers see the chapters that are genuinely released to them, and the early access window decides when that is. A chapter approved without a scheduled date takes the moment of approval as its early access date, and general availability follows seven days later. Only the Visionary tier and above count as early access readers. A Founder-tier backer waits those seven days like everyone else, so a post written on approval day reaches a small slice of your audience immediately and the rest a week on.

You and your contributors get every chapter that is not a draft, free, with no age gate and with the early access date ignored. That is how you hand a test build to someone.

Nobody gets a draft. To show unfinished work, put the chapter into testing and add the person as a contributor instead. See [Testing and contributors](/en/getting-started/testing-and-contributors/).
