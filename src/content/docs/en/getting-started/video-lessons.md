These are four short screen recordings: the story, the editor, characters and variables, and the chapter. Under each one is a link to the page with the exact labels and limits.

You can also read these pages inside TalePort:

- Open the **Help** menu in the editor header and choose **Documentation**.
- On any page, press **F1** to open the panel. **?** works too, unless you're typing in a field.
- Press **Esc** to close it.

Each lesson in the panel has a **Watch on YouTube** link.

## Lesson 1: Creating a story

<iframe class="video-lesson" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/DE5AgeGlwso?rel=0"
        title="Lesson 1: Creating a story" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen
        allow="accelerometer; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture"></iframe>

You create a story with a title, description, cover, genre, tags and at least one chapter. The lesson shows the two steps of the **Create New Epos** dialog, **The basics** and **Details**. Only the title and genre are required. The lesson ends in the editor, in an empty **Chapter 1**.

[Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/DE5AgeGlwso) · [Read more: Creating a story](/en/getting-started/creating-a-story/)

## Lesson 2: Editor tour

<iframe class="video-lesson" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/_SXjc4tCP_I?rel=0"
        title="Lesson 2: Editor tour" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen
        allow="accelerometer; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture"></iframe>

The editor shows the graph of one chapter.

- Drag nodes and components in from the palette on the left.
- The selected node opens on the right under **Components**, **Outputs**, **Preview** and **Notes**.
- The bottom panel holds what the whole story shares: **Characters**, **Variables**, **Stats** and **Global Events**.

The editor needs a desktop computer or a tablet in landscape. To replay its twelve-step walkthrough, choose **Show editor tutorial** in the **Help** menu.

[Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/_SXjc4tCP_I) · [Read more: The story graph](/en/story-editor/story-graph/)

## Lesson 3: Characters, stats, and variables

<iframe class="video-lesson" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/SzYJnSrgB3Q?rel=0"
        title="Lesson 3: Characters, stats, and variables" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen
        allow="accelerometer; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture"></iframe>

Characters decide who speaks. Variables store the state of the story. An event component changes a variable, and a requirement on a transition reads it. A character's stats are variables on their sheet.

[Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/SzYJnSrgB3Q) · [Read more: Variables](/en/story-editor/variables/)

## Lesson 4: Building the story

<iframe class="video-lesson" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/KZISUtXtd6c?rel=0"
        title="Lesson 4: Building the story" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen
        allow="accelerometer; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture"></iframe>

The reader sees a node as one screen. Its components are the text, dialogue, image and sound, and its output decides where the reader goes next.

The lesson connects nodes with the three most common outputs:

- a continue, where the story doesn't branch
- a choice, where it does
- an ending that finishes the chapter, not only the current branch

[Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/KZISUtXtd6c) · [Read more: Transitions and choices](/en/story-editor/transitions-and-choices/)
