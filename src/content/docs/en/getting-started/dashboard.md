The **Dashboard** shows where each of your chapters stands in review. Open it from the side menu. It says: "Track your chapter submissions and publications."

![the Dashboard with the tabs Needs Attention (0), In Review (3) and Published (5); the In Review tab lists three chapters, each with its story and author, the time it was submitted and a withdraw button, and the last row is opened to show the cover, story and chapter descriptions, price, early access date, age rating, content tags and AI labels](/screens/en/getting-started/dashboard.png)

## The three tabs

Each tab has a counter, so you can see at a glance whether something needs you.

| Tab | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs Attention** | Chapters a reviewer sent back to you. Start here. |
| **In Review** | Chapters you have submitted that a reviewer holds. |
| **Published** | Chapters that have passed review and are live. |

A live chapter you have resubmitted appears on both **Published** and **In Review**: readers still see the approved version while the new one waits.

When a tab is empty, it says why. **Needs Attention** reads "All caught up. No chapter needs an author action right now."

## Each row

A row shows the chapter title, then the story and the author. When a reviewer has taken the chapter, it also says who. On the right is how long ago the chapter was submitted. Hover over it to see the exact date and time.

Click a row to open it. The opened row shows the same things a reviewer sees:

- the cover,
- the story description and the chapter description,
- the price, or **Free**,
- the early access date, or **Immediately** when you did not schedule one,
- the age rating, content tags and the AI labels you declared.

Use this to check what you submitted without opening the editor.

## What you can do from a row

- On **Needs Attention**, the **Open** button opens the chapter in the editor. The comment button, **View comments**, shows the reviewer's feedback and how many comments there are. See [Working with review feedback](/en/publishing/review-feedback/).
- On **In Review**, the arrow button, **Withdraw from review**, takes the chapter back. See [The review process](/en/publishing/review-process/).
- On **Published**, there is only a check mark. A published chapter is changed from the editor.
