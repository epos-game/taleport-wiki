Your story is a list of chapters, and readers play them from the top down. The **Chapters** dialog is where you add chapters, change their order and open them in the editor.

## Opening the dialog

Open **Chapters** in the editor, or press **Manage** on the Chapters card of the story's detail page. Both show the same list. The dialog says: "Readers play the chapters in this order."

![the Chapters dialog: the Chapter order list with a published chapter locked at the top and a draft chapter below it marked Next to publish and Active, an Add chapter 3 row, and the How chapters work panel](/screens/en/getting-started/chapter-management.png)

## What each row shows

- **The title and description** of the chapter.
- **The status** on the right, for example **Published** or **Draft**.
- **A lock** in place of the drag handle on a chapter that can't be moved. Hover over it and it says "Published and in-review chapters can't be moved."
- **A drag handle** on a draft, which you can move.
- **Next to publish** on the first chapter that is not yet live or in review. It is the chapter that has to go through review next.
- **Active** on the chapter that is open in the editor right now.
- **Edit** (the pencil) and **Delete** (the bin).

Click a row to open that chapter in the story editor.

## Adding a chapter

The last row, for example **Add chapter 3**, adds a chapter at the end of the list: "Continue the story where chapter 2 ends. It starts as a draft, not visible to readers." In a story without chapters, the row says "Start your story with its first chapter."

A new chapter needs a title and a description before you can create it. Later you can change both with the pencil.

## Changing the order

Drag a draft to a new place to change the order. The new order is saved when you drop it. If it can't be saved, the dialog says "The new chapter order could not be saved."

Only drafts move. Published chapters and chapters in review keep their place at the top, and you can't drag a draft above them. A chapter has to be reviewed before the one after it, so the order you set is also the order in which the chapters go to readers. See [Release order](/en/getting-started/story-structure-and-chapters/#release-order).

## Editing and deleting

- A chapter in review can't be edited or deleted. The pencil is greyed out: "Chapters in review cannot be edited." Withdraw the chapter from review to change it.
- You can't delete the last chapter: "A story must have at least one chapter."
- Deleting asks for confirmation. It can't be undone from the dialog.

For what deleting a published chapter does, see [Story structure and chapters](/en/getting-started/story-structure-and-chapters/#adding-renaming-and-deleting-chapters).
