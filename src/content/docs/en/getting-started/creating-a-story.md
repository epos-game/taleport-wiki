A **story** is the container for your work. It holds what all its chapters share: title, cover, genre, tags, age recommendation, characters, variables and global events. Every story has at least one **chapter**, and readers buy and read chapters.

## Create New Epos

Open **My Stories** and choose **Create Story**. The **Create New Epos** dialog has two steps.

1. **The basics.** Enter a **Story Title** (required, 200 characters at most) and a **Description** (formatted text, 1000 characters at most). If you go over a limit, **Next** is blocked, but nothing is cut.
2. **Details.** Pick a **Genre** (required). You can also add **Tags**. They help readers find the story.

**Next** stays disabled until the step is complete. **Create Epos** turns on once you've filled in the title and genre. You can change everything later on the story's detail page or in **Global Settings** in the editor header (the cog next to the book icon).

![the Create New Epos dialog with the story title filled in](/screens/en/getting-started/create-story-dialog.png)

This dialog doesn't set the cover. Use the **Thumbnail** button on the story's detail page. You can't publish a chapter until the story has a cover. See [Cover image](/en/media/cover-image/).

A new story starts with one empty chapter, **Chapter 1**, and TalePort opens the editor right away.

## Locked content

Publishing a chapter locks its graph. Some story-wide items get locked too. See [Published content restrictions](/en/publishing/published-content-restrictions/).

- **Characters:** a character is locked once it speaks a line in a live chapter. A character in a node without a line stays editable.
- **Global events:** all of them are locked as soon as any chapter is live, even events that chapter doesn't fire. The variable each event targets is locked with it.
- **Variables:** a variable is locked when a live chapter changes it in an event or checks it in a requirement. The stat variables of a locked character are locked too.
