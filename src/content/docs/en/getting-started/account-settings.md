**Account settings** in the side menu opens the **Settings** page. It says: "Manage your account and author profiles." The page has three sections.

![the Settings page with the Account owner section (name fields and a private-name note), the Payouts & billing section with a connected bank account and the tax and billing details, and the Author profiles section listing two profiles, the first one marked active](/screens/en/getting-started/account-settings.png)

## Account owner

This is you as a person, not the name readers see. It shows your login email and, once the address is confirmed, a **Verified** label. You can change your **First Name** and **Last Name**. A **Save** button appears when you have changed something. The first name can't be empty.

"Your legal name is private - readers only see your author profiles."

## Payouts and billing

This section has two rows: the bank account and the tax and billing details. Once the bank account is saved, the row says **Connected**. The account number is hidden, and the eye icon shows it. Use the pencil to change a row and the bin to remove it.

You need both rows before you can charge for a chapter. What goes in them and when the money arrives is on the page [Payout process](/en/monetization/payouts/).

## Author profiles

An author profile is a public identity you publish stories under. It is what readers see, so it carries a name, a nickname, a picture and a bio, and not your legal name. One account can have several profiles, for example a pen name for each genre. Every story belongs to one profile.

The list shows each profile with its picture and nickname. The check mark on a picture marks the **Active** profile, the one you are working as. Press **Manage**, or click a profile, to open the dialog.

### Managing profiles

![the Manage profiles dialog: the list of profiles on the left with a New profile button, and on the right the avatar, Author name, Nickname, a Bio editor and the Links, with Cancel and Save at the bottom](/screens/en/getting-started/manage-profiles.png)

The dialog says: "Public identities you publish stories under." The list is on the left, and the profile you pick opens on the right.

| Field | What goes in it |
| --- | --- |
| Picture | The profile's avatar. The cross removes it. |
| **Author name** | The name readers see. |
| **Nickname** | A handle that shows as `@EposTeam`. It can't contain spaces. |
| **Bio** | A short text about you, with bold, italic, underline, alignment and indent. Up to 10,000 characters. |
| **Links** | Up to 10 links, each with an optional label. A link must start with `http://` or `https://`. Use **Add link** for another. |

To create a profile, press **New profile**. A new profile needs an author name or a nickname before you can save: "Enter an author name or nickname to save." **Save** applies your changes and **Cancel** drops them.

Before you can publish a chapter, the profile needs a display name, a bio and an avatar. See [Publishing requirements](/en/publishing/publishing-requirements/).

### Switching profiles

You work as one profile at a time. You see only that profile's stories and statistics. To change it, use **Switch profile** in the editor. The editor asks "Switch to …?" and warns: "You'll only see stories and stats for … until you switch again."

### Deleting a profile

The bin appears when you point at a profile, as long as you have more than one. You can't delete your only profile: "You can't delete your only profile."

:::caution[Deleting a profile deletes its stories]
"Deleting this profile will also delete all of its stories. This can't be undone." The dialog lists the stories that will go. Check it before you confirm.
:::
