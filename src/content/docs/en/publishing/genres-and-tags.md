Your story needs one genre and at least one tag. You pick both from lists that TalePort maintains. The genre is the broad category. Tags describe the story in more detail, and readers filter by them.

You can't submit a chapter until the story has both.

- **When you create the story:** only the genre is required.
- **In the publish dialog:** the **Metadata** step has the fields **Genre** and **Tags**. Here you need at least one tag too.
- **In the Validation checklist:** the rows are *Genre selected* and *At least one tag*.

## Genre

Pick exactly one of fifteen genres: **Fantasy**, **Science Fiction**, **Horror**, **Thriller**, **Crime**, **Romance**, **Adventure**, **Drama**, **Comedy**, **Historical**, **Mystery**, **War**, **Western**, **Poetry** and **Educational**.

Genre and tag names are translated. A reader browsing in Czech sees Horor and Gotický román where you see Horror and Gothic Novel. If a translation is missing, the Czech name shows.

## Tags

You can pick from 109 tags, each with a Czech and an English name. Pick at least one. There is no maximum.

| Kind | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Subject and form of story | Detective, Romantasy, Dark Romance, Gothic Novel, Fairy Tale, Mythology, Fanfiction, Satire |
| Setting and era | Medieval, Antiquity, Modern Era, Futuristic, Post-Apocalyptic, Urban, Rural, Nordic, Far East |
| Tone | Grimdark, Tragic, Humor, Psychological, Philosophical, Realistic |
| Aesthetic | Steampunk, Cyberpunk, Dieselpunk, Solarpunk |
| Protagonist and narration | Female Protagonist, Multiple Protagonists, Silent Protagonist, Unreliable Narrator, First Person, Second Person |
| Structure and play | Branching, Linear, Skillchecks, Combat, Survival, Roleplay, Riddles |
| Media and format | Audiobook, Video, Animated, ASMR, Comics, Poetry, Short Stories, Novella |
| Audience | For Toddlers, Family, Educational, LGBT |

To add a tag, type a few letters in the tag field and click the match. The tag moves under the field. You can remove it there.

- A reader who filters by several tags sees stories with any of them, not only with all of them. Each tag puts your story in one more filter.
- TalePort can retire a tag. A retired tag stays on your story and keeps working. You can't add it to another story.

## Choosing tags

- Tag what readers notice while reading. If something shaped your writing but does not show, skip the tag.
- A tag that does not fit shows your story to readers who wanted something else.
- Tags are not content tags. A tag says what the story is about. A [content tag](/en/publishing/content-labels/) warns what is in it. Horror as a genre, as a tag and as a content tag are three different things.
