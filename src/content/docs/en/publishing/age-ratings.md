The age rating has four values: **7+**, **12+**, **16+** and **18+**. You set it in the **Classification** step of the publish dialog. It tells readers what to expect. It also decides who can see your story.

You cannot submit a chapter until you pick a rating.

## The main rule

The strongest content in the story sets the rating. It is not an average. One scene of realistic violence sets the rating for the whole story, even if the rest is gentle.

- The rating belongs to the story, not to a chapter.
- It also covers chapters you have not written yet.
- A high rating never makes prohibited content allowed. Even 18+ permits nothing listed in [Article I](/en/publishing/content-rules/#cr-i).

## Raising and lowering

- **Raise** the rating at any time.
- **Lower** it only until your first chapter goes live.
- Once a chapter is live, lower options are greyed out. The tooltip says *The age recommendation can't be lowered once a chapter is live.*
- A live chapter that is back under review still counts as live.

## Checking and saving

The rating is checked in the **Classification** step, not in the **Validation** checklist.

- **Next** stays disabled until you pick a rating and tick the human-authorship checkbox. The tooltip says *Choose an age rating and confirm human authorship before continuing.*
- TalePort checks again when you submit. If the rating is missing, it says *An age recommendation is required before publishing.*
- **Next** also saves. The rating, the content tags and the AI declaration go to the story at once. Closing the dialog does not undo them.
- Until a chapter is live, you can reopen the step and choose again. The rating saved when your first chapter goes live becomes the lowest you can use.

## The categories

Under the option you pick, the app shows a one-line summary. The full definitions are in [Article IV](/en/publishing/content-rules/#cr-iv) of the Content Rules. Each rating below links to its definition.

| Rating | In the app | May contain | Must not contain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [7+, everyone](/en/publishing/content-rules/#cr-iv-1) | *Suitable for everyone, including young readers.* | Mild fantasy or cartoon violence, mild peril, themes suitable for children, innocent affection or romance | Strong language, realistic violence, sexual themes, drug use or other mature themes |
| [12+, mild themes](/en/publishing/content-rules/#cr-iv-2) | *Mild themes, brief violence, or fantasy peril.* | Mild action or combat, moderate fantasy violence, tension and peril, occasional mild profanity, romance and kissing, mild references to mature themes | Explicit sexual content, strong graphic violence, anything meant only for adults |
| [16+, mature themes](/en/publishing/content-rules/#cr-iv-3) | *Strong language, violence, or mature themes.* | Strong language, realistic violence, horror, alcohol or drug themes, grief, addiction, mature relationships, non-explicit sexual references | Pornographic or explicit sexual content |
| [18+, adults only](/en/publishing/content-rules/#cr-iv-4) | *Adult content only.* | Stronger adult content: strong violence, disturbing or psychologically intense themes, strong language, drug or alcohol themes, nudity, sexual themes, non-explicit depictions of consensual sexual activity between adults | Pornographic content, which stays prohibited at every rating |

## Who sees your story

Readers outside your team see stories rated for their age band or lower. The band comes from the birthday on their account. This applies to browsing and to opening a story directly.

| Reader | Highest rating they can see |
| --- | --- |
| Under 12 | 7+ |
| 12 to 15 | 12+ |
| 16 to 17 | 16+ |
| 18 and over | 18+ |
| No birthday on the account | 7+ |

Readers aged 18 and over see every rating, including a story with no rating. Admins and reviewers do too. Younger readers never see an unrated story.

## If the rating looks wrong

- **During review.** The reviewer checks the rating under [CR-V.1](/en/publishing/content-rules/#cr-v-1). If it is too low, they send the chapter back with notes. Then another review round follows.
- **After publication.** A reader can report the story as **Misclassified**, one of seven reasons. TalePort reviews the report and does not notify the author.

[CR-VI.1](/en/publishing/content-rules/#cr-vi-1) says what TalePort may do next: require a corrected rating, hide the content or restrict the account. You do none of this in the app.
