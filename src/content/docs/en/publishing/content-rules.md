Every story on TalePort has to follow these rules. A reviewer uses them to decide whether a chapter passes review.

They cover everything you submit: story text, illustrations, photographs, audio, music, voice recordings, video and any other media. By submitting a chapter for review or publication, you confirm that you have read the rules and agree to them.

:::note[Citing a rule]
Every rule has a permanent number such as `CR-I.1`. A reviewer can quote it in a review note. The address <https://docs.epos.games/r/CR-I.1> leads to the rule in Czech or English, even after the wording changes. The Classification step of the publish dialog links here under **Review the content rules**. The app keeps no copy of its own and shows exactly this text.
:::

## Article I. Prohibited content {#cr-i}

None of the following is allowed on TalePort or in EPOS, at any age recommendation.

### `CR-I.1` Sexual content involving minors {#cr-i-1}

Any sexual depiction, sexualisation or exploitation of a real or fictional person shown as under 18 is prohibited. This covers text, images, audio and every other form, explicit or implied. Fictional, illustrated and animated characters are not an exception.

### `CR-I.2` Pornographic content {#cr-i-2}

Pornographic content, and content whose main purpose is sexual arousal, is prohibited. A story for adults may contain mature relationships, sexual themes, nudity and non-graphic references to consensual sexual activity between adults. The narrative must call for it, and the story must be rated 18+. Explicit pornographic description, images, video and audio are prohibited at every rating.

### `CR-I.3` Non-consensual sexual content and sexual exploitation {#cr-i-3}

Content is prohibited if it promotes, glorifies or shows sexual violence, coercion or exploitation for sexual gratification. Sexual violence and abuse may be part of a narrative if you handle them responsibly and do not write them to titillate. A story that deals with them needs a fitting age recommendation.

### `CR-I.4` Hate speech and discrimination {#cr-i-4}

Content is prohibited if it promotes, encourages or glorifies hatred, violence or discrimination against a person or group. This covers race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sex, gender, age, sexual orientation and gender identity. Your story may show discrimination, prejudice, extremist characters or historical atrocities, but the work itself must not endorse them.

### `CR-I.5` Extreme violence and gore {#cr-i-5}

Gratuitous torture, mutilation or extreme graphic violence is prohibited when it is written for shock or for the enjoyment of suffering. Violence, death, war, horror and their consequences are allowed when the narrative carries them and the age recommendation fits.

### `CR-I.6` Self-harm and suicide {#cr-i-6}

Content is prohibited if it encourages, promotes, teaches or helps a reader to commit suicide or self-harm. Suicide, self-harm, mental distress and similar subjects may be themes in your story, but the story must not encourage the behaviour and the age recommendation must fit.

### `CR-I.7` Dangerous or illegal activities {#cr-i-7}

Content must not give usable instructions for serious illegal or dangerous activity. That includes making illegal weapons or explosives, producing illegal drugs, committing serious crimes and creating malicious software. Crime, weapons and drugs may appear in fiction. Only content that works as a practical manual is prohibited.

### `CR-I.8` Harassment, threats and abuse {#cr-i-8}

Content is prohibited if its main purpose is to threaten, harass, bully or intimidate a real person. Conflict, threats and abusive behaviour between characters belong to fiction and are allowed when the narrative and the age recommendation carry them.

### `CR-I.9` Privacy and personal information {#cr-i-9}

Do not publish another person's private or sensitive information without a lawful basis or that person's permission. Doxxing is prohibited. So is publishing private contact details, financial information, login credentials or similar sensitive information.

### `CR-I.10` Impersonation and deceptive content {#cr-i-10}

Content must not intentionally impersonate a real person or organisation in a way that could mislead readers about its origin or authenticity. Fiction, parody and satire involving real people may be allowed if it is reasonably clear that the work is fictional or satirical and it breaks no other rule.

### `CR-I.11` Unlawful content {#cr-i-11}

Content that breaks applicable law is prohibited, even when no rule above describes it.

## Article II. Intellectual property and third-party content {#cr-ii}

### `CR-II.1` Rights to submitted content {#cr-ii-1}

Submit only content you created yourself or hold the rights, permissions or licences to use and publish. This applies to text, illustrations and photographs, music and sound effects, voice recordings, video, logos and trademarks, and any other protected material.

### `CR-II.2` Third-party intellectual property {#cr-ii-2}

Do not use someone else's copyrighted works, characters, fictional universes, music, artwork, trademarks or other protected material. The exception is when you hold the rights or the law permits the use. Material being public on the internet does not mean you may publish it.

### `CR-II.3` Responsibility of the author {#cr-ii-3}

You are responsible for the rights to everything you submit. Where it is reasonably necessary, TalePort may ask for a licence, a permission or proof of ownership. We may reject or remove content when we have reasonable grounds to believe it infringes third-party rights.

## Article III. AI-generated content {#cr-iii}

AI may produce some of the media in a story. A human must write the story text.

### `CR-III.1` AI-generated text is prohibited {#cr-iii-1}

You must not submit or publish text generated by generative AI as part of a story. This covers story prose, dialogue, narration, choice text and anything else readers see as part of the story. You may use AI tools for spelling, grammar checking, proofreading or translation. A human must have written the underlying text, and the tool must not generate or substantially rewrite the creative content. The Classification step asks you to tick a checkbox: "I confirm that the text of this story was created by a human author and was not generated by artificial intelligence." You cannot submit the chapter for review until you tick it.

### `CR-III.2` AI-generated media {#cr-iii-2}

AI-generated and AI-assisted illustrations, images, audio, music and video may be allowed. You declare them in the Classification step under **AI-generated content**. It offers four chips: AI Image, AI Music, AI Speech and AI Video. You can select only media your story actually contains. The declaration also feeds the recommended price for the chapter.

### `CR-III.3` Rights and responsibility {#cr-iii-3}

You are fully responsible for all AI-generated and AI-assisted content you submit. Using AI does not remove your duty to check that the content follows these rules and that you may publish it. AI output is prohibited if it infringes intellectual property rights, imitates a real person deceptively or harmfully, or breaks any other rule here.

### `CR-III.4` False disclosure {#cr-iii-4}

If you intentionally fail to declare AI-generated content where a declaration is required, we may reject or remove the content. In repeated or serious cases, we may also restrict your author account.

## Article IV. Age recommendations {#cr-iv}

Every story has one age recommendation. You set it in the Classification step of the publish dialog, based on the most restrictive part of the story, not its average. No recommendation makes otherwise prohibited content acceptable.

Once any chapter of the story is live, the recommendation is a floor. You can raise it. Every lower option is disabled with the tooltip "The age recommendation can't be lowered once a chapter is live." Content tags work the same way. You cannot remove a saved tag, but you can still add new ones.

### `CR-IV.1` 7+, everyone {#cr-iv-1}

May contain mild fantasy or cartoon violence, mild peril, themes suitable for children, and innocent affection or romance. Should not contain strong language, realistic violence, sexual themes, drug use or other mature themes.

### `CR-IV.2` 12+, mild themes {#cr-iv-2}

May contain mild action or combat, moderate fantasy violence, tension and peril, occasional mild profanity, romance and kissing, and mild references to mature themes. Must not contain explicit sexual content, strong graphic violence or other content meant only for adults.

### `CR-IV.3` 16+, mature themes {#cr-iv-3}

May contain strong language, realistic violence, horror, alcohol or drug-related themes, grief, addiction and other difficult subjects, mature relationships, and non-explicit sexual references. Must not contain pornographic or explicit sexual content.

### `CR-IV.4` 18+, adults only {#cr-iv-4}

May contain stronger adult content. This includes strong violence, disturbing or psychologically intense themes, strong language, drug or alcohol-related themes, nudity, sexual themes, and non-explicit depictions or descriptions of consensual sexual activity between adults. Pornographic content stays prohibited at every age recommendation. An 18+ story reaches only readers whose date of birth on their account puts them in the adult band. A reader with no date of birth sees 7+ stories and nothing else.

## Article V. Content review and publication {#cr-v}

### `CR-V.1` Review before publication {#cr-v-1}

Submitting content does not mean it will be published. A chapter submitted for publication goes to a reviewer first, and readers can download it only after that. A reviewer may check:

- compliance with these rules
- whether the age recommendation fits
- the required AI declaration
- intellectual property rights
- technical requirements
- anything else that applies to publishing on TalePort and EPOS

If the reviewer wants changes, they reject the chapter with notes and it comes back to you to edit. Approval is not final. TalePort may review the content again and may act later if a violation is found or reported.

## Article VI. Reporting and moderation {#cr-vi}

### `CR-VI.1` Reports and moderation actions {#cr-vi-1}

Readers can report a story from EPOS. A report picks one of seven reasons (explicit content, hate speech, extreme gore, illegal content, misclassified, spam, other). The reader may add up to 1000 characters of their own words. Reports are filed against the story, not against a single chapter. TalePort also reviews content on its own, including content submitted for publication.

Depending on the situation, TalePort may:

- request changes
- change an age recommendation or require you to correct it
- refuse publication
- temporarily hide content, restrict access to it, or remove it
- suspend publishing privileges
- restrict or terminate an author account

The action depends on the nature and severity of the violation. Where it is appropriate, you will be told what action was taken against your content and why.

## Article VII. Appeals {#cr-vii}

### `CR-VII.1` Requesting a review of a decision {#cr-vii-1}

If you believe your content was wrongly rejected, restricted or removed, ask for a review of the decision through the contact method TalePort provides. Give enough detail to identify the story or chapter, and say why the decision should be reconsidered. TalePort reviews reasonable appeals. It may uphold, change or reverse the original decision.

## Article VIII. Consequences of violations {#cr-viii}

### `CR-VIII.1` Enforcement {#cr-viii-1}

We usually handle a minor or first-time violation by rejecting or hiding the affected content and giving you the chance to correct it. Serious or repeated violations may lead to:

- removal of individual chapters or stories
- removal of several or all of your published works
- temporary suspension of publishing privileges
- suspension or termination of the author account

We may act on a particularly serious violation immediately, without a prior warning. Where the law requires it, or where it is necessary to protect readers or third parties, TalePort may keep relevant information and report content or activity to the appropriate authorities.

## Article IX. Changes to these rules {#cr-ix}

### `CR-IX.1` Updates to these rules {#cr-ix-1}

We may update these rules when TalePort, EPOS, the law, distribution platform requirements or our moderation standards change. For a material change, we may ask you to read and accept the updated rules before you submit more content for publication.
