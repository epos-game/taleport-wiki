These are the rules every story on TalePort has to follow: what you may publish, how it has to be labelled, and what happens when a rule is broken. They decide whether a chapter clears review, so reading them before you submit is cheaper than being rejected. A siege chapter where a captured scout is interrogated is fine at 16+ with the Violence and Blood content tags. The same scene written to dwell on the torture falls under `CR-I.5`, and no age recommendation makes it publishable.

The rules cover everything you submit: story text, illustrations, photographs, audio, music, voice recordings, video and any other media. Submitting a chapter for review or publication confirms that you have read them and agreed to them.

:::note[Citing a rule]
Every rule has a permanent identifier such as `CR-I.1`. A reviewer can quote that identifier in a review note, and <https://docs.epos.games/r/CR-I.1> resolves to the rule in Czech or English even after the wording changes. The Classification step of the publish dialog links here under **Review the content rules**; the app downloads this page rather than keeping its own copy of it, so what you read in TalePort is this text.
:::

## Article I. Prohibited content {#cr-i}

None of the following is permitted on TalePort or in EPOS, at any age recommendation.

### `CR-I.1` Sexual content involving minors {#cr-i-1}

Any sexual depiction, sexualisation or exploitation of a real or fictional person portrayed as under 18 is prohibited. This covers text, images, audio and every other form, explicit or implied. Fictional, illustrated and animated characters are not an exception.

### `CR-I.2` Pornographic content {#cr-i-2}

Pornographic content, and content whose primary purpose is sexual stimulation, is not permitted. A story written for adults may contain mature relationships, sexual themes, nudity and non-graphic references to consensual sexual activity between adults where the narrative calls for it and the story is classified 18+. Explicit pornographic description, images, video or audio are not permitted at any rating.

### `CR-I.3` Non-consensual sexual content and sexual exploitation {#cr-i-3}

Content that promotes, glorifies or presents sexual violence, coercion or exploitation for sexual gratification is prohibited. Sexual violence and abuse may be part of a narrative when they are handled responsibly and not written to titillate. A story that deals with them needs an age recommendation that reflects it.

### `CR-I.4` Hate speech and discrimination {#cr-i-4}

Content that promotes, encourages or glorifies hatred, violence or discrimination against a person or group is prohibited. The characteristics this covers include race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sex, gender, age, sexual orientation and gender identity. Your story may still depict discrimination, prejudice, extremist characters or historical atrocities, so long as the work itself does not endorse them.

### `CR-I.5` Extreme violence and gore {#cr-i-5}

Gratuitous torture, mutilation or extreme graphic violence written for shock or for the enjoyment of suffering is prohibited. Violence, death, war, horror and their consequences are permitted where the narrative carries them and the age recommendation matches.

### `CR-I.6` Self-harm and suicide {#cr-i-6}

Content that encourages, promotes, instructs or assists a reader in committing suicide or self-harm is prohibited. Suicide, self-harm, mental distress and similar subjects may be themes in your story when it does not encourage the behaviour and the age recommendation matches.

### `CR-I.7` Dangerous or illegal activities {#cr-i-7}

Content must not provide actionable instructions for serious illegal or dangerous activity. That includes manufacturing illegal weapons or explosives, producing illegal drugs, committing serious crimes and creating malicious software. Crime, weapons and drugs may appear in fiction; what is prohibited is content that works as a practical manual.

### `CR-I.8` Harassment, threats and abuse {#cr-i-8}

Content whose primary purpose is to threaten, harass, bully or intimidate a real person is prohibited. Conflict, threats and abusive behaviour between characters belong to fiction and are permitted where the narrative and the age recommendation carry them.

### `CR-I.9` Privacy and personal information {#cr-i-9}

Do not publish another person's private or sensitive information without a lawful basis or that person's permission. Doxxing and the publication of private contact details, financial information, authentication credentials or similar sensitive information are prohibited.

### `CR-I.10` Impersonation and deceptive content {#cr-i-10}

Content must not intentionally impersonate a real person or organisation in a way likely to deceive readers about its origin or authenticity. Fiction, parody and satire involving real people may be permitted when it is reasonably clear that the work is fictional or satirical and it breaks no other rule here.

### `CR-I.11` Unlawful content {#cr-i-11}

Content that violates applicable law is prohibited even when no rule above describes it.

## Article II. Intellectual property and third-party content {#cr-ii}

### `CR-II.1` Rights to submitted content {#cr-ii-1}

Submit only content you created yourself or hold the rights, permissions or licences to use and publish. This applies to every part of a story: text, illustrations and photographs, music and sound effects, voice recordings, video, logos and trademarks, and any other protected material.

### `CR-II.2` Third-party intellectual property {#cr-ii-2}

Do not use copyrighted works, characters, fictional universes, music, artwork, trademarks or other protected material belonging to someone else unless you hold the necessary rights or applicable law permits that use. Material being publicly available on the internet does not make it free to publish.

### `CR-II.3` Responsibility of the author {#cr-ii-3}

You are responsible for holding the rights to everything you submit. TalePort may ask you to produce a licence, a permission or proof of ownership where that is reasonably necessary. Content may be rejected or removed where we have reasonable grounds to believe it infringes third-party rights.

## Article III. AI-generated content {#cr-iii}

AI may assist with some of the media in a story. The story itself has to be the work of its human author.

### `CR-III.1` AI-generated text is prohibited {#cr-iii-1}

Text generated by generative AI must not be submitted or published as part of a story. This covers story prose, dialogue, narration, choice text and anything else presented to readers as part of the story. You may use AI tools for spelling, grammar checking, proofreading or translation, provided a human wrote the underlying text and the tool does not substantially generate or rewrite the creative content. The Classification step states this as a checkbox you have to tick: "I confirm that the text of this story was created by a human author and was not generated by artificial intelligence." Nothing can be submitted for review until you do.

### `CR-III.2` AI-generated media {#cr-iii-2}

AI-generated and AI-assisted illustrations, images, audio, music and video may be permitted. You declare them in the Classification step under **AI-generated content**, which offers four chips: AI Image, AI Music, AI Speech and AI Video. Only media your story actually contains can be selected. The declaration also feeds the recommended price for the chapter, which is one more reason it has to be truthful.

### `CR-III.3` Rights and responsibility {#cr-iii-3}

You remain fully responsible for all AI-generated and AI-assisted content you submit. Using AI does not move the duty to check that the content complies with these rules and that you may publish it. AI output that infringes intellectual property rights, imitates a real person deceptively or harmfully, or breaks any other rule here is prohibited.

### `CR-III.4` False disclosure {#cr-iii-4}

Intentionally failing to declare AI-generated content where a declaration is required may result in the content being rejected or removed. Repeated or serious cases may also result in restrictions on your author account.

## Article IV. Age recommendations {#cr-iv}

Every story carries one age recommendation, set in the Classification step of the publish dialog. Choose the category that fits the most restrictive part of the story rather than its average. No recommendation makes otherwise prohibited content acceptable.

Once any chapter of the story is live, the recommendation becomes a floor. You can raise it, and every lower option is disabled with the tooltip "The age recommendation can't be lowered once a chapter is live." Content tags harden the same way: a tag already saved cannot be taken off, though new tags can still be added.

### `CR-IV.1` 7+, everyone {#cr-iv-1}

May contain mild fantasy or cartoon violence, mild peril, themes suitable for children, and innocent affection or romance. Should not contain strong language, realistic violence, sexual themes, drug use or other mature themes.

### `CR-IV.2` 12+, mild themes {#cr-iv-2}

May contain mild action or combat, moderate fantasy violence, tension and peril, occasional mild profanity, romance and kissing, and mild references to mature themes. Must not contain explicit sexual content, strong graphic violence or other content intended exclusively for adults.

### `CR-IV.3` 16+, mature themes {#cr-iv-3}

May contain strong language, realistic violence, horror, alcohol or drug-related themes, grief, addiction and other difficult subjects, mature relationships, and non-explicit sexual references. Must not contain pornographic or explicit sexual content.

### `CR-IV.4` 18+, adults only {#cr-iv-4}

May contain stronger adult content, including strong violence, disturbing or psychologically intense themes, strong language, drug or alcohol-related themes, nudity, sexual themes, and non-explicit depictions or descriptions of consensual sexual activity between adults. Pornographic content remains prohibited regardless of age recommendation. An 18+ story reaches only readers whose date of birth on their account puts them in the adult band, and a reader who has given no date of birth is shown 7+ stories and nothing else.

## Article V. Content review and publication {#cr-v}

### `CR-V.1` Review before publication {#cr-v-1}

Submitting content does not guarantee publication. A chapter submitted for publication goes to a reviewer before readers can download it. A reviewer may weigh compliance with these rules, whether the age recommendation fits, the required AI declaration, intellectual property rights, technical requirements and anything else that applies to publishing on TalePort and EPOS. A reviewer who wants changes rejects the chapter with notes, and it comes back to you editable. Approval does not close the file: TalePort may review the content again and may act later if a violation is discovered or reported.

## Article VI. Reporting and moderation {#cr-vi}

### `CR-VI.1` Reports and moderation actions {#cr-vi-1}

Readers can report a story from EPOS. A report picks one of seven reasons (explicit content, hate speech, extreme gore, illegal content, misclassified, spam, other) and may carry up to 1000 characters of the reader's own words. Reports are filed against the story rather than against a single chapter. TalePort also reviews content on its own initiative, including content submitted for publication.

Depending on the situation, TalePort may request changes, change or require correction of an age recommendation, refuse publication, temporarily hide content, restrict access to it, remove it, suspend publishing privileges, or restrict or terminate an author's account. Which of those applies depends on the nature and severity of the violation. Where it is appropriate, you will be told what action was taken against your content and why.

## Article VII. Appeals {#cr-vii}

### `CR-VII.1` Requesting a review of a decision {#cr-vii-1}

If you believe your content was wrongly rejected, restricted or removed, you can ask for the decision to be reviewed through the contact method TalePort provides. Give enough detail to identify the story or chapter, and say why you think the decision should be reconsidered. TalePort reviews reasonable appeals and may uphold, modify or reverse the original decision.

## Article VIII. Consequences of violations {#cr-viii}

### `CR-VIII.1` Enforcement {#cr-viii-1}

In most cases a minor or first-time violation is handled by rejecting or hiding the affected content and giving you the chance to correct it. Serious or repeated violations may lead to the removal of individual chapters or stories, the removal of several or all of your published works, temporary suspension of publishing privileges, or suspension or termination of the author account. A particularly serious violation may be acted on immediately, without a prior warning. Where the law requires it, or where it is necessary to protect readers or third parties, TalePort may preserve relevant information and report content or activity to the appropriate authorities.

## Article IX. Changes to these rules {#cr-ix}

### `CR-IX.1` Updates to these rules {#cr-ix-1}

These rules may be updated in response to changes to TalePort, EPOS, applicable law, distribution platform requirements or our moderation standards. Where a change is material, authors may be asked to read and accept the updated rules before submitting further content for publication.
