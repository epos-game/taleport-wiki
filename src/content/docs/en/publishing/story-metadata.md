Metadata is what a reader sees before opening your story: title, cover, description, genre, tags and classification badges.

Some of it belongs to the story and covers every chapter. The rest belongs to the chapter you are publishing. The publish dialog stops you while any required part is empty.

## Story-level fields

- Title, up to 200 characters.
- Description, up to 1000 characters.
- Cover image. See [Cover image](/en/media/cover-image/), and [Images](/en/media/images/) for formats and rights.
- One [genre](/en/publishing/genres-and-tags/) and at least one [tag](/en/publishing/genres-and-tags/).
- Classification: the [age recommendation](/en/publishing/age-ratings/), the [content tags](/en/publishing/content-labels/), the AI-generated content declaration and the human-authorship confirmation. All four belong to the story. They also cover chapters you have not written yet.
- Contributors, each credited with one or more contributions. Six are ready-made: Author, Translator, Publisher, Editor, Storyteller and Illustrator. You can also type your own. See [Testing and contributors](/en/getting-started/testing-and-contributors/).

Before any chapter can go to review, the story needs a title, description, cover, genre and at least one tag. TalePort also checks your author profile. See [Publishing requirements](/en/publishing/publishing-requirements/).

## Chapter-level fields

- Title, up to 200 characters.
- Description, up to 1000 characters.

You cannot pass the **Metadata** step without both.

## The 1000-character limit

You write both descriptions in the rich-text editor. The counter measures the text as stored, formatting included. Paragraph breaks, bold text and links also count toward the limit.

So a description that looks like 800 characters can be over the limit. The counter under the editor then turns red, and the step will not save.

## Filling it in while you publish

The **Metadata** step shows a **Live preview** card beside the **Story details** fields. **This chapter** has its own block underneath.

- With no cover, the card shows a coloured stand-in with the title and genre. Until you fill them in, it reads "Untitled story" and "No genre yet".
- Once there is a cover, the cover fills the card and the title and genre disappear.

The step has seven items:

1. story title
2. description
3. cover
4. genre
5. tags
6. chapter title
7. chapter description

**Next** stays disabled until you fill in all seven. The tooltip says why: "Fill in the required chapter details before continuing." Classification is not part of this step. It has its own step.

You set the cover with a tile, not a field. The preview card has a tile that reads **Set up the cover**, or **Edit the cover** once there is one. It opens the [cover editor](/en/media/cover-image/). After you save, you return to this step.

While the story has no cover, a warning under the card says: "Your story has no thumbnail. It is what readers see everywhere except inside the story."

**Next** also saves. It writes the story and the chapter at once, so what you typed stays even if you close the dialog two steps later. The **Classification** step saves the same way. A content tag you tick is on the story right away.

### Contributors step

This step decides who keeps access to the story after publishing. You can also change what each contributor is credited for. Credits of a contributor with an Epos account save as soon as you change them.

The publish dialog cannot add a new contributor. It cannot change the credits of someone with no Epos account either. Do both under Contributors on the story itself.

## How readers rate your story

Readers rate each chapter thumbs up or thumbs down. These votes add up to the story's rating.

## Rights on the cover

The cover must meet the same rights requirements as the rest of the story, [CR-II.1](/en/publishing/content-rules/#cr-ii-1).

The AI-generated content declaration, [CR-III.2](/en/publishing/content-rules/#cr-iii-2), covers the media inside the story: images on nodes, character avatars and global-event images. The cover is not one of them, so you do not switch on the AI Image option for it, even if AI made it.
