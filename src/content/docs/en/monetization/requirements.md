To sell a chapter, you need two things: the Qualified author stage and complete payout details. Until both pass, the charge toggle in the publish dialog stays greyed out and you can submit only free chapters.

Free chapters need neither, so you can publish those from the start.

## Becoming Qualified

**One approved free chapter with at least 20 minutes of content makes you Qualified.**

- The 20 minutes must be in one chapter. Three seven-minute chapters don't add up.
- The chapter must be free, approved and published. A chapter that's still in review hasn't qualified you yet.
- The chapter's total content counts, not one playthrough. TalePort adds up everything in the graph, including branches a reader never sees and unreachable nodes.
- TalePort measures every approved free chapter, not only the first. If chapter 1 misses the threshold, chapter 4 can still qualify you.

For a text-only chapter, 20 minutes is 20,000 characters, a little over eleven standard pages.

The chapter list shows a **Total reading time** for each chapter. This is the figure TalePort measures. The longest path and the average path don't count here.

### Counted content

TalePort measures each node once, using the first of these that the node has:

1. **Text**, counted by length. A thousand characters is a minute.
2. **Voice-over**, if the node has no text. This is the running time of the recordings on the text and dialogue lines.
3. **Cutscene video**, if the node has neither.

This means two things:

- Narration adds nothing to a node that already has text. A node with 2,000 characters and a two-minute recording counts as two minutes, not four.
- Background music, ambient sound and looping background video don't count, because they have no single running time.

## Payout and billing details

Qualification isn't enough. TalePort refuses a paid submission while your billing is incomplete. You need both parts: a bank account and a tax and billing profile. See [Payout process](/en/monetization/payouts/).

You can delete either part later from the same page, but then you lose paid publishing again.

The publish checklist always has a **Billing and payout information** row with an **Open billing settings** button. Until both parts are complete, this row stays open, whatever the chapter's price. A free submission still goes through; only a paid one is stopped.

## Ask for qualification

A free chapter is the usual route, but not the only one. The **Pricing** step has a link for writing to support@epos.games to ask to be qualified.

TalePort may enable paid publishing without the standard requirement, at its own discretion. Article V of the Terms and Conditions says clearly that you have no entitlement to this.

## Author stages

TalePort tracks your author stage, and the paid-publishing check reads it. You can't set it yourself.

The **Pricing** step says you can charge "once you're a Qualified author". If a paid submission gets past the dialog, it comes back with "You must be a Qualified author before you can publish paid content."

| Stage | How you reach it |
| --- | --- |
| Registered | You have an account. |
| In production | You created a story, or a chapter of yours entered review. |
| Published | One of your chapters is published. |
| Qualified | A qualifying free chapter was published, or TalePort marked you as Qualified. |
| Active partner | Your first paid chapter was published while you were Qualified. |
| Terminated | TalePort ended the partnership. |
| Expired | Nothing in TalePort moves an author to this stage. |

## Qualification duration

Meeting the free-chapter requirement doesn't give you a permanent right to sell. Article V.4 of the Terms and Conditions says TalePort may refuse, suspend or withdraw monetization, especially for a breach of the Terms, the content rules, third-party rights, the law or the distribution platforms' rules.

In the app this is the Terminated stage. You then fail the paid-publishing check, just as at the Registered stage. Free chapters aren't affected.

## Non-qualifying chapters

The check runs automatically after a chapter is approved. If your chapter is free, published and has 20 minutes of content, but your stage hasn't changed, write to support and they will look into it.
