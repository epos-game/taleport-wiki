EPS is the in-app currency. Readers buy it with real money and spend it to unlock chapters. You set every price in EPS. 1 EPS is 1 CZK, roughly €0.04.

A reader tops up once, then buys chapters from the balance. TalePort records the real money behind each top-up and works out your earnings from it, meaning the net revenue from your sales.

## Where credits come from

- An in-app purchase through Apple or Google.
- A card payment through Stripe.
- A redemption code, bought on the web and entered in the app. It works once and expires 90 days after it's issued. It belongs to the order, not to a person, so the first reader to enter it gets the credits.
- Credits granted by a TalePort admin. They count toward your earnings like any other top-up.

Tax, the store's commission and the payment processing fee come off the gross amount. What's left is the net revenue. That is your earnings, before your share is applied.

## How credits are spent

TalePort tracks each top-up separately, with the money paid and the currency. A purchase uses the oldest top-up first, then the next one. One sale of your chapter can draw on two or three top-ups, and each adds its own share of net revenue.

For readers, a credit is fixed: 1 EPS is 1 CZK. For you, it has no fixed value. It depends on where the top-up was bought, what tax applied and what the store took.

If everything arrived in one currency and the story has earned credits, the statistics page shows an **Average credit value** row. See [Story statistics](/en/monetization/statistics/).

## Refunded top-ups

If a reader gets a refund for a top-up, only the unspent credits from that top-up are lost. Credits already spent on your chapters stay spent, and the chapters stay in the reader's library. The money side shows on your statement; see [Payout process](/en/monetization/payouts/).

## What a chapter costs

A chapter costs its price in credits, rounded up to a whole credit. A reader buys it once and keeps it in their library. Free chapters cost nothing and go into the library without a purchase.

If the balance is too low, nothing is charged. The app shows how many credits the chapter needs and how many the reader has.

What happens to access when a story is hidden or removed:

- If you hide a story, everyone loses download access except TalePort's reviewers and admins. That includes readers who already own a chapter.
- If an author deletes their account, the story is only removed from the catalogue. Owners keep their access.
