Every story in your library has a statistics page. Open it with the **Statistics** button in the action row at the top of the story's detail page, next to **Edit Details**, **Thumbnail** and **Open graph**.

The page shows which chapters readers opened, bought or took for free, and how much money is recorded for the purchases. The numbers are totals, and no reader is named. Only the story's author can open the page.

## The four headline numbers

- **Plays** counts chapters that have been opened. Each reader counts once per chapter.
- **Unique buyers** is how many different readers bought at least one paid chapter.
- **Paid chapters bought** counts purchases, not people. A reader who buys three chapters counts three times.
- **Free chapters downloaded** counts free chapters added to a library, once per reader per chapter. Three free chapters taken by one reader add three.

A few details about these numbers:

- A play is recorded the first time a reader opens a chapter they own. Re-reading adds nothing. So the number is one play per reader per chapter.
- Copies taken by your story team, a TalePort admin or a partner count too.
- A paid chapter given to your team free of charge counts in neither download tile, because no purchase was recorded and the chapter isn't free.

## Earnings

The earnings card and monthly chart appear after the first purchase or free download. The card has these rows:

- **Estimated earnings** is the net revenue recorded against your sales, split by the currency each reader paid in.
- **Credits earned** shows the credits readers spent on this story, in EPS. This figure is gross, not your share.
- **Average credit value** is earnings divided by credits. It's shown only when every sale came in one currency and the story has earned credits.
- **Store commission deducted** is the fixed note "Apple / Google, 15–30 %". This is the range the two stores publish.
- **Estimated gross** is what readers paid before tax and fees. It comes from the top-ups their credits came out of.

While there is nothing to show, the card says "No completed purchases yet." Usually this means nobody has bought anything. The text also appears when a purchase can't be matched to the top-ups it was paid from.

The card also notes that the figure is only a guide. It doesn't include refunds or chargebacks processed after a purchase.

The amount shown is the full net revenue recorded for those sales, before your share is taken. Your share comes from the price list or your own agreement, and the Terms and Conditions say when it's paid. See [Author royalties](/en/monetization/royalties/).

## Over time and by chapter

**Purchases and downloads** is a chart with one column for each of the last twelve months. Each column is split into **Bought** and **Downloaded free**, with the month's total above it. The chart counts only purchases and downloads, not money or plays.

**By chapter** lists every chapter by sequence number, drafts included. A chapter you have never submitted shows with a count of 0. Each row shows:

- the price, or "Free",
- a count labelled **Bought by** or **Downloaded by**,
- a **Credits** figure, which is a dash for free chapters,
- a bar.

The row counts add up to the tiles above.

The bar compares the row's count to chapter 1's count and stops at 100%, so it shows how many readers drop off. If chapter 1 is free and the rest are paid, chapter 1 shows downloads and the others show buyers, so read the bars knowing that they compare different units.

The note under the list mentions bundle purchases. They don't apply to you, because every purchase is for exactly one chapter.

## Reading the numbers

- Credits earned can be higher than Estimated earnings. If a purchase can't be matched to the top-ups it was paid from, the credits count but no money is added.
- This page doesn't subtract refunds and chargebacks.
- The free and paid split uses the chapter's current setting, so switching it changes past numbers:
  - A paid chapter turned free shows its earlier buyers under Free chapters downloaded, next to Paid chapters bought, and its Credits cell becomes a dash.
  - A free chapter turned paid loses its earlier downloads from the tile.
