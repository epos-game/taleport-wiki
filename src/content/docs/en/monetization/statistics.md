Every story in your library has its own statistics page, opened from the Statistics button that sits beside Edit Details and Open graph on the story's detail page. It gathers what readers did with that story: chapters they opened, chapters they bought, free chapters they took, and the money recorded behind the purchases. The page is yours alone, and no reader is ever named on it. Say chapter 1 of The Lighthouse Contract went out free 412 times and chapter 2 sold 47 copies at 89 EPS: this is where you find the 47, the 4,183 credits behind them, and a bar saying chapter 2 reached 11% of the people who started chapter 1.

## The four headline numbers

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Plays** | Chapters that have been opened, counting each reader once per chapter. |
| **Unique buyers** | How many different readers bought at least one paid chapter. |
| **Paid chapters bought** | Purchases, not people. One reader buying three chapters counts three. |
| **Free chapters downloaded** | Free chapters added to a library, counted once per reader per chapter. |

A play is recorded the first time a reader opens a chapter they already own, and never again for that chapter. Re-reads, replays and trips back through the other branches add nothing. A reader who finishes your story five ways registers exactly as much as one who opened it once and left, which makes the tile's own subtitle, "reading sessions started", a promise the number does not keep.

Free chapters downloaded counts library entries, not heads. Three free chapters taken by the same reader add three. Copies taken by your story team, and by a TalePort admin or partner, land here like anyone else's, because a free chapter enters their library the same way. A paid chapter handed to your team for nothing is counted in neither tile: no purchase was recorded, and the chapter is not free.

## Earnings

The earnings card and the monthly chart stay hidden until the story has its first purchase or its first free download. Until a purchase completes, the card reads "No completed purchases yet."

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Estimated earnings** | Net revenue recorded against your sales, split by the currency each reader paid in. |
| **Credits earned** | The credits readers spent on this story, in EPS. Gross, not a share. |
| **Average credit value** | Earnings divided by credits. Shown only when every sale came through one currency and the story has earned credits. |
| **Store commission deducted** | The fixed note "Apple / Google, 15–30 %". A published range, not your measured rate. |
| **Estimated gross** | What readers paid before tax and fees, taken from the top-ups their credits came out of. |

Two caveats come from the page itself and both hold: the figure is indicative, and it does not yet account for refunds or chargebacks processed after a purchase. A third one is not written anywhere on the page. The amount shown is the full net revenue recorded for those sales, with no revenue share applied to it. How much of it is yours comes from the price list or your own agreement, and when it reaches you is set out in the Terms and Conditions. See [Author royalties](/en/monetization/royalties/).

Credits granted by an admin do carry money behind them. A grant records a gross amount, a tax rate and the RevenueCat fee, so credits spent out of one raise both Credits earned and Estimated earnings. See [Credits](/en/monetization/credits/).

## Over time, and by chapter

Purchases and downloads is a column per month across the last twelve, each one stacked from Bought and Downloaded free, with that month's total printed above it. No money and no plays go into it. Volume only.

By chapter lists every chapter of the story by sequence number, drafts included, so a chapter you have never submitted sits in the list with a count of 0. Each row carries the price or "Free", a count labelled Bought by or Downloaded by, a Credits figure (a dash for free chapters) and a bar. Those counts add up to the tiles above, since a reader buys a chapter once and takes a free one once.

The bar compares that row's count to chapter 1's own count and is capped at 100 %. Read it as drop-off and nothing more. When chapter 1 is free and the rest are paid, the baseline is downloads while the rows measured against it are buyers, so the two sides of the comparison are not the same unit.

![the By chapter list on a story's statistics page, each row showing the sequence number, title, price, how many readers bought or downloaded that chapter, the credits it earned, and a bar relative to chapter 1](/screens/en/monetization/statistics-by-chapter.png)

The note under that list mentions bundle purchases being spread across the chapters they cover. Ignore it. TalePort has no bundles, and every purchase is for exactly one chapter.

## Reading it honestly

- Free downloads against paid purchases is the comparison the page lays out for you. Both tiles cover the same story and the same window, so the gap between them is about your free chapter rather than about traffic.
- Credits earned can run ahead of Estimated earnings. When a purchase cannot be matched back to the top-ups it was paid from, the credits still count and nothing is attributed, so the earnings line does not move.
- Refunds and chargebacks are not subtracted here at all. That is the reason the card calls its own amount indicative.
