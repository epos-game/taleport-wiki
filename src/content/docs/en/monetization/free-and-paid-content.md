You choose free or paid for each chapter. Every chapter is sold on its own. TalePort records your choice when you submit the chapter for review.

- A **free** chapter goes into the reader's library when they take it.
- A **paid** chapter costs [EPS](/en/monetization/credits/).
- The app won't let a reader buy a chapter they already own.

## Do you need a free chapter?

No. A paid submission checks your author stage and billing details, not chapter numbers, so a Qualified author can charge for chapter one.

:::caution[Qualifying chapter stays free]
The free chapter you qualified with must stay free, unless you agree otherwise with TalePort. This comes from Article V of the Terms and Conditions. Make sure you keep to it yourself.
:::

## Set a price

1. Open the **Pricing** step of the publish dialog.
2. Switch on **Do you want to charge for this chapter?**. The price field, the slider and the presets open up.
3. Keep the price TalePort recommends from the chapter's content, or change it within the allowed range.

If you switch the toggle off, the price goes back to 0. See [Pricing](/en/monetization/pricing/).

The toggle stays greyed out until you're a Qualified author. TalePort refuses a paid submission while your payout and billing details are incomplete. See [Monetization requirements](/en/monetization/requirements/).

## Change it later

You can resubmit a published chapter and switch it between free and paid. The version readers already have stays available during the review.

Readers who own a chapter keep it. If you make a free chapter paid, nobody loses it and nobody is charged.

## Early access

Early access lets backers read a chapter first. Everyone else gets it 7 days later.

On the **Early access** step, answer **Do you want to start early access at a specific time?**

- If the toggle is off, no date is set.
- If the toggle is on, the calendar opens on the earliest date you can pick, and backers can read the chapter from that date.
- The earliest date is 14 days from now, because a review can take that long. The calendar greys out earlier dates, and TalePort refuses an earlier date when you submit.
- If you leave the date empty, approval sets it to the moment of approval, so backers get the chapter straight away.

Who counts as a backer:

- Readers with the Backer role. Redeeming a crowdfunding pledge code grants that role at every tier, Founder included.
- The Founder tier alone counts as ordinary public access. Without the Backer role, it doesn't open early access.

:::caution[Early access date stays]
Resubmitting can't change this date. On a chapter that's already live, TalePort keeps the original date. Whether the chapter is free or paid, and its price, can change.
:::
