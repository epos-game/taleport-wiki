A royalty is your share of the real money sitting behind the credits a reader spends on your chapter. TalePort records what every top-up was worth once tax and fees came off, and each chapter sale that spends from that top-up takes a proportional slice of it. A 500 EPS top-up bought through the App Store for 499 CZK is not 499 CZK of revenue: VAT takes about 87, Apple's commission about 150 and the RevenueCat fee 5, leaving roughly 257 CZK behind those credits. Unlock *The Drowned Chapel* for 79 EPS out of that top-up and 79 of the 500 credits are consumed, so about 41 CZK is booked against the sale, and your contractual share comes out of that 41.

Earnings are held and reported in the currency each reader paid in, never in credits. If readers paid in three currencies, the story's statistics page lists three amounts side by side.

**Credits earned** on the same page is a different number: the credits readers spent on the story. Gross, and not a share of anything.

## What comes off before your share

A payment never arrives whole. Three deductions are recorded on the top-up itself, before anything is attributed to a chapter:

- Tax, at the rate the store reports for that purchase.
- The store's commission, on a purchase made through Apple or Google.
- The RevenueCat fee, a flat 1% of the gross amount.

What remains is the net revenue of that top-up. Credits are consumed oldest top-up first, so one purchase can reach into two of them and take a slice from each at that top-up's own rates. Your earnings are therefore not the price multiplied by the number of buyers, and the gap moves from reader to reader.

Credits that came from a code bought on the web are recorded the same way, except that the deductions are the order's VAT and payment fee and the RevenueCat fee is zero.

The Terms and Conditions list deductions the on-screen figure cannot reflect, because they land later: refunds and chargebacks, currency conversion differences, and other direct costs of the transaction.

## Read the figure as a ceiling

**Estimated earnings** is the net revenue recorded against your sales. The card calls it your estimated share of net revenue, but no percentage has been applied to it. Your share is set by TalePort's current price list or by your individual agreement, under Article V.6 of the Terms and Conditions, and it is taken out of this number.

Two things show up as activity and add nothing here:

- **Free copies.** People on your story team, TalePort admins and TalePort partners receive chapters without paying, paid ones included. Reviewers do not: a reviewer who is not on your team buys a paid chapter like any other reader.
- Sales TalePort could not match to a top-up. When the credits spent cannot be traced to a top-up on record, nothing is attributed, and a later sweep may file a placeholder with every amount at zero. The purchase still counts in credits earned.

Credits an admin granted a reader are not on that list. A grant records a gross amount, a tax rate, a store fee and the RevenueCat fee, so a chapter bought with granted credits earns from it like any other purchase.

## Where to see it

Per story, on its [statistics page](/en/monetization/statistics/): the earnings card and the per-chapter list. Read the per-chapter list first. It shows which chapters people pay for.

The twelve-month chart on the same page plots purchases and free downloads. There is no money in it.

## Statements and payment

TalePort prepares a **monthly statement** of net revenue and your share of it. Payment follows no later than the end of the fourth calendar month after the month the revenue was generated. [Payout process](/en/monetization/payouts/) covers the carry-forward threshold and what you have to set up before any of it can reach you. Article V of the Terms and Conditions, in the app, has the full wording.
