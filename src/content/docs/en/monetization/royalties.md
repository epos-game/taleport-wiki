A royalty is your share of the real money behind the credits a reader spends on your chapter. Here's how it works:

1. TalePort records what each top-up was worth after tax and fees.
2. Each chapter sale paid from that top-up takes a proportional slice of it.
3. Your share is taken from the total of those slices.

**Earnings** means net revenue from your sales throughout the help: the amount after tax and fees, before your percentage share is applied. Your royalty is then your share of those earnings.

Earnings are held and shown in the currency each reader paid in, never in credits. If readers paid in three currencies, the story's statistics page shows three amounts side by side.

**Credits earned** on the same page is a different number. It's the credits readers spent on the story, so it's gross, not your share.

## Deductions before your share

TalePort records three deductions on each top-up before crediting any of it to a chapter:

- tax, at the rate the store reports for that purchase,
- the store's commission, on a purchase made through Apple or Google,
- the payment processing fee.

What remains is that top-up's net revenue. Credits are spent from the oldest top-up first, so one purchase can use two top-ups and take a slice from each at each top-up's own rates. That's why your earnings aren't the price multiplied by the number of buyers.

Credits from a code bought on the web are recorded the same way. TalePort deducts the order's VAT and payment fee.

The Terms and Conditions also list deductions that the on-screen figure can't show, because they come later:

- refunds and chargebacks,
- currency conversion differences,
- other direct costs of the transaction.

## What the figure means

**Estimated earnings** is the net revenue recorded for your sales. No percentage has been applied to it yet.

Your share comes from TalePort's current price list or your individual agreement, under Article V.6 of the Terms and Conditions, and it's taken from this number.

Free copies add nothing to your earnings. Your story team, TalePort admins and TalePort partners get chapters, paid ones included, without paying.

## Where to see earnings

Earnings show per story on its [statistics page](/en/monetization/statistics/):

- an earnings card,
- the **By chapter** list, which shows which chapters people pay for.

The twelve-month chart on the same page shows purchases and free downloads. It shows no money.

## Statements and payment

TalePort prepares a **monthly statement** of net revenue and your share. Payment follows by the end of the fourth calendar month after the month the revenue was earned, at the latest.

[Payout process](/en/monetization/payouts/) explains the carry-forward threshold and what you must set up before money can reach you. Article V of the Terms and Conditions, in the app, has the full wording.
